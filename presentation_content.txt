Slide 2: About Us
Title: Who We Are
*   **Overview:** IRIS Foundation is an educational and social initiative working among backward and neglected communities in North India.
*   **Mission:** To awaken the hopes of children in forgotten villages by providing Quranic and formal education, skill development, and social care.
*   **Vision:** A future where no child is left behind due to poverty or geography, and every village becomes a center of learning and mercy.
*   **Our Values:**
    *   Compassion: Serving with mercy.
    *   Impact: Focusing on real change.
    *   Transparency: Accountability in all projects.
    *   Excellence: Striving for quality in service.

---

Slide 3: Impact & Reach
Title: Where We Work
*   **Focus Area:** We serve villages in Punjab and other North Indian states that lie beyond the main roads.
*   **The Need:** Addressing the lack of strong schools, health facilities, and opportunities in these regions.
*   **Key Interventions:**
    *   Education Support
    *   Relief Work
    *   Food Programs
    *   Clean Drinking Water Projects

---

Slide 4: Holistic Community Development
Title: Our Services & Initiatives
*   **Education:** Establishing academies and schools for child education.
*   **Training:** Leadership training programs for youth.
*   **Cultural Centers:** Community hubs in over 40 villages.
*   **Relief:** Winter relief drives and emergency assistance.
*   **Feeding:** Extensive food programs for the hungry.
*   **Drops of Life:** Projects ensuring access to clean drinking water.

---

Slide 5: Key Educational Programs
Title: Nurturing Future Leaders
*   **Hifzul Qur’an Model Academy:** Residential academy combining Scripture memorization with mainstream schooling.
*   **Micro Schools (Maktabs):** Network of 500+ children receiving basic Islamic learning and academic support near their homes.
*   **Rabbani Fellowship:** Training 200+ young men and women to become community leaders and teachers.
*   **Village Education Centers:** 40+ centers attached to mosques offering evening classes and mentoring.

---

Slide 6: Imam Rabbani Center (MIR)
Title: Center for Education & Guidance
*   **Overview:** A pioneering institution in Sirhind, Punjab.
*   **The Vision:** To produce balanced, purpose-driven individuals by blending Islamic scholarship with modern academic education.
*   **Inspiration:** Modeled after the educational ecosystem of Markaz Knowledge City and the spiritual legacy of Hazrat Mujaddid Alf-Thani.
*   **Goal:** Integrating "Deen" (Faith) and "Dunya" (World) to create confident, spiritually aware leaders.

---

Slide 7: MIR Academic Framework
Title: A Holistic Approach
*   **Islamic Studies:** Classical subjects including Quran, Hadith, Fiqh, and Aqidah.
*   **Modern Curriculum:** Academics aligned with national education boards.
*   **Tarbiyah & Adab:** Mentorship focusing on daily discipline and spiritual development.
*   **Leadership Training:** Cultivating critical thinking, ethical reasoning, and community service.

---

Slide 8: MIR Programs Offered
Title: Educational Pathways
1.  **Islamic Secondary Program (5 Years):**
    *   For students after Grade 7.
    *   Combines Islamic sciences with school-level academics.
2.  **Bachelor's in Usul al-Din (4 Years):**
    *   For students after Higher Secondary.
    *   Specializations in Fiqh, Hadees, and Theology + Global Leadership Training.
3.  **Advanced Leadership Program (1 Year):**
    *   Refining skills for Islamic Studies graduates.
