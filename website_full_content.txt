## 2. ABOUT US
**Our Story:**
*   Subtitle: Touching hearts and changing lives in the forgotten villages of North India.
*   Who We Are: IRIS Foundation is an educational and social initiative working among backward and neglected communities in North India. Through different projects, the foundation supports children who lose their chance to study because of poverty, distance, and family responsibilities.
*   Our Reach: Our teams reach villages that lie beyond the tarred roads, where schools are weak, health facilities are limited, and children rarely see opportunities outside traditional work. In these places IRIS Foundation becomes a door to learning, care, and hope.

**Mission & Vision:**
*   **Mission:** To awaken the hopes of children in neglected villages and give them Quranic and formal education, skill development, and social care so that they can stand on their own feet with dignity.
*   **Vision:** Rural communities where no child is left behind because of poverty or geography, and where every village becomes a center of learning, responsibility, and mercy.

**Our Values:**
1.  **Compassion:** Serving with mercy and care for every child and family.
2.  **Impact:** Focusing on real change in villages, not just numbers.
3.  **Transparency:** Working with clarity and accountability in all projects.
4.  **Excellence:** Striving for quality in teaching, management, and service.

**Join Our Journey:**
*   Your support helps us keep village schools alive, run maktabs, distribute relief, and bring clean water to families that have been forgotten.

## 3. IMAM RABBANI CENTER (MIR)
**Overview:**
*   Title: Imam Rabbani Center for Education & Guidance
*   Subtitle: The symbol signifies that knowledge is the bedrock of all social, cultural, and environmental sustainability in the universe.

**Founder's Message (Dr. Muhammed Abdul Hakkim Azhari):**
*   "At the heart of every revival lies a vision—an unwavering belief that transformation begins with education. The Imam Rabbani Centre for Education & Guidance was born from such a vision. Rooted in the historic soil of Sirhind, our mission is to cultivate a generation of young Muslims who are equally grounded in faith and prepared for the modern world.
*   We refuse to see a divide between sacred knowledge and worldly success; instead, we strive to nurture leaders who embody the balance, clarity, and purpose found in our Deen.
*   Inspired by the spiritual legacy of Hazrat Mujaddid Alf-Thani and the educational brilliance of the Kerala model, we offer a system that integrates Islamic values, academic excellence, and ethical leadership."

**About the Centre:**
*   Location: Sirhind, Punjab.
*   Description: A pioneering institution offering a harmonious blend of Islamic scholarship and modern academic education.
*   Inspiration: Modeled after Markaz Knowledge City in Kerala and the spiritual legacy of Hazrat Mujaddid Alf-Thani.

**Core Components:**
1.  **Islamic Studies:** Classical subjects including Qur'an, Hadith, Fiqh, and Aqidah.
2.  **Modern Curriculum:** Subjects aligned with national education boards.
3.  **Tarbiyah & Adab:** Daily discipline, mentorship, and spiritual development.
4.  **Leadership Training:** Critical thinking, ethical reasoning, and community service.

**Programs Offered:**

    *   Highlights: Specialization in Hanafi Fiqh/Hadees/Theology, Foreign Language Proficiency, Global Leadership Training, Pathway to Al-Azhar.
3.  **Advanced Leadership Program (1 Year):**
    *   Eligibility: For Islamic Studies Graduates.
    *   Description: Refined skills and broadened perspectives for graduates.

**Why Choose Us:**

## 4. PROGRAMS (KEY INITIATIVES)
**Overview:**
*   Focused programs that turn village children and youth into tomorrow’s leaders.
*   Each program addresses an urgent village need: Quranic and formal education, youth guidance, and cultural spaces.

**List of Programs:**
1.  **Hifzul Qur’an Model Academy:**
    *   Description: A residential academy in rural Punjab where children memorize the Qur’an while continuing their mainstream studies in a safe, supportive environment.
    *   Stats: Established 2022 · 1 campus · first batch of 15 students.
2.  **Micro schools Imam Rabbani (Maktabs):**
    *   Description: A network of village maktabs providing basic Islamic learning, Qur’an reading, and academic support near students' homes.
    *   Stats: Multiple village maktabs · 500+ children reached.
3.  **Rabbani Fellowship:**
    *   Description: Intensive fellowship training young men and women to become community leaders, teachers, and social workers.
    *   Stats: 200+ fellows trained · Active in 15+ states.
4.  **Village Education & Cultural Centers:**
    *   Description: Centers attached to mosques hosting evening classes, mentoring sessions, and cultural programs.
    *   Stats: 40+ centers active in Punjab and nearby states.

## 5. SERVICES
### A. Education
*   Title: Empowering Through Knowledge
*   Description: Education is the most effective way to break the cycle of poverty. Initiatives span from formal Islamic education to village-level maktabs.
*   **Hifzul Quran Model Academy:** Comprehensive Islamic education with Quran memorization. Free boarding, meals, and materials.
*   **Village Education & Maktab Programs:** Community learning centers providing literacy, numeracy, and religious studies. Staffed by trained local teachers.

### B. Relief
*   Title: Emergency Assistance (Disaster Response)
*   Description: Rapid response capability for natural disasters, emergencies, or economic hardships.
*   **Key Areas:**
    *   Disaster Response (Immediate assistance).
    *   Essential Supplies (Food, clothing, medical).
    *   Rehabilitation (Long-term support).
    *   Emergency Shelter (Temporary housing).

### C. Cultural Center
*   Title: Preserving Heritage
*   Description: Vital community hubs where traditions are preserved and bonds strengthened.
*   **Features:** Cultural Events, Community Gatherings, Educational Programs, Multi-purpose Hall.
*   **Opportunity:** "Build a Cultural Center" - Contribution options available (e.g., ₹1,400 per sq ft).

### D. Drops of Life (Water)
*   Title: Clean Water for All
*   Description: Ensuring every community has access to safe, clean drinking water.
*   **Initiatives:**
    *   Water Wells (Bore wells).
    *   Water Tanks (Storage).
    *   Purification (Clean drinking systems).
    *   Maintenance (Ongoing upkeep).

### E. Feeding Food
*   Title: No One Goes Hungry
*   Description: Regular food distribution drives, community kitchens, and student meal programs. Focus on sustainable solutions like community gardens.
*   **Stats:** 500+ Meals Daily, 10+ Communities, 365 Days a Year.

### F. Training
*   Title: Skills for Self-Reliance
*   Description: Vocational training programs equipping individuals with marketable skills.
*   **Programs:**
    *   Tailoring & Stitching (Garment making for women).
    *   Computer Literacy (Basic to advanced).
    *   Handicrafts (Traditional arts).
    *   Agriculture Training (Modern farming).
